# Approved narrative update — 6 November 2026

This records Ethan's explicit update to the original spec. It supersedes conflicting earlier draft dialogue, not unrelated mechanics or level layouts.

## Five-floor progression

1. **The Prisoner:** introduction to moving, hiding, fighting and escaping. Miro is isolated, unsure of himself, and presented by the institution as dangerous. The sick Keeper shows that a uniform offers no immunity.
2. **The Symptoms:** continue onboarding while showing indiscriminate confinement and sick staff ordered to remain at work. Reveal cruelty without explaining the final twist.
3. **The Order:** the checkpoint hack remains required. Root is revealed in overheard dialogue. Former-Keeper records expose Miro's complicity and confirm the brother relationship. Hacking expresses defiance of imposed rules.
4. **The Ward:** expose coercive research and real harm to staff. The lab-origin file is still sealed. Plant most of the C4: two charges at the west support, two at the lower support, one at the original fourth-sigil support. These use existing walkable tiers; no new rooms or structural changes. Five charges here versus one on each other floor means five of nine total.
5. **The Brother:** the tower physician, Miro's brother, blocks escape. Mandatory lab records and dialogue before the fight reveal Miro's innate immunity, the tower's creation of the pathogen, and its attempt to reproduce his resistance through involuntary experiments. The brother believes confinement protects Miro and preserves a cure. His motives do not erase the betrayal. His fear of admitting his life's work served a lie informs his refusal to let go.

The institution is the central antagonist. Its sickness and hierarchy carry the social symbolism; characters do not deliver an explanatory lecture. Miro's arc moves from confusion and survival toward self-determination. The brother is both an agent of harm and a victim of the institution's promises. The illness and people still trapped inside remain real; destroying the tower does not receive a morally simplifying epilogue.
