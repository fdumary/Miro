# Cutscene and menu ambience sources

- `rain_loop.wav`, `thunder_near.wav`, `thunder_far.wav`: cut from "Rain + Long Thunder" by WuxiaScrub,
  https://opengameart.org/content/rain-long-thunder (CC0). Rain loop is 0.5-20.5 s with a 2 s
  crossfade seam; near thunder is 21.5-33.0 s (crack at the start, for flash sync); far thunder is the 25-37 s tail, softened.
  Levels match the synthesized files they replaced (rain RMS -26 dB; thunder near -20.5, far -27.5).
- Everything else in this folder is synthesized (`tools/synth_cine_audio.py`).
